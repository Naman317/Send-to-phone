Step 1

Create Your Telegram Bot

You need a Telegram bot to send files. It only takes 1 minute.

1 Open Telegram and search for @BotFather
2 Send the command /newbot
3 Follow the prompts — give your bot a name and username
4 Copy the token BotFather gives you (looks like 123456:ABC-DEF…)

Step 2

Find Your Chat ID

This tells the bot where to send your files.

1 Open Telegram and search for @userinfobot
2 Press Start — it will reply with your info
3 Copy the Id number from the reply
4 Also send a message to your bot first so it can reach you
Files over 50 MB cannot be sent — this is a Telegram Bot API limit.
